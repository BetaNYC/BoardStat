What's changed

This page lists the changes to BoardStat that a reader would notice: new pages, new data, data fixes, and corrections when BoardStat showed something wrong. Newest first. BoardStat's 311 data refreshes every night, and those refreshes are not listed. The "What changed since" box on a district page lists the 311 requests that changed overnight; this page lists changes to BoardStat itself.

  • History begins: 2017-05-18, the day BoardStat's GitHub repository opened. Entries before November 2026 cover the original BoardStat, one per release, from its README and issues. Corrections made before November 2026 may be missing.
  • Data refresh: nightly from NYC Open Data, at 5:30 AM, with a second try at 8:30 AM if the first fails

BetaNYC uploads BoardStat 2.0 to www.beta.nyc/boardstat/ by hand, so a change reaches the site at the next upload. Entries since November 2026 are dated by the day the change was merged on GitHub. BetaNYC's nyc-311-mcp server reads BoardStat's data files, so a change to BoardStat's data can change its answers too. To report a bug or ask for a new feature, email boardstat@beta.nyc or open an issue on GitHub.

The data paused for two days, and the nightly refresh now tries twice

November 9, 2026

Tags: Data

On November 8 and 9, 2026, NYC Open Data turned away the 5:30 AM refresh, so BoardStat kept showing data built on the morning of November 7 until a refresh run by hand on the evening of November 9. When the first try fails, the refresh now tries again at 8:30 AM.

Who it affects: Anyone who used BoardStat on November 8 or 9, 2026. The About page always shows when the data was last built.

Tags: Data

The original BoardStat showed data from early 2025 without saying it was out of date

January 14, 2026

Tags: Data, Correction

The City's Office of Technology and Innovation told BetaNYC that BoardStat's connection to NYC Open Data had stopped working, and that its data had not refreshed since early 2025. Until this date the pages did not say so. On January 14, 2026, a notice went on the home page saying the data could no longer be refreshed. The five borough pages, where the numbers were, carried no notice until they began sending visitors to BoardStat 2.0 on November 5, 2026.

If you used the old numbers: Numbers from the original BoardStat taken from early 2025 until November 5, 2026, stop in early 2025. Re-check them in BoardStat 2.0, which covers 2020 on.

Who it affects: Anyone who used the original BoardStat from early 2025 on.

Some requests had been shown under the wrong community board

July 11, 2018

Tags: Data, Correction

BoardStat showed each request under the community board listed in the City's 311 data. For some requests, that board did not match where the request was. Requests from Marble Hill showed up under Manhattan Community Board 8, reported in September 2017. Community Board 1 received requests that belonged elsewhere, noted for Manhattan on May 14, 2018, and for every borough later that month, when the issue counted tens of thousands of requests across complaint types such as Street Light Condition and Heat/Hot Water. On this date the README recorded that NYC's Open Data team had addressed these problems.

If you used the old numbers: Counts for Manhattan Community Board 8, or for Community Board 1 in any borough, taken from the original BoardStat from 2017 until as late as November 2019 may include requests from other districts. The record does not say how many requests each board gained. The original BoardStat no longer runs, so to re-check a count from those years, use the City's 311 Service Requests 2010 to 2019 dataset on NYC Open Data and check each request's location, not only its community board field. BoardStat 2.0 does not use that field: it places each request by its location.

Who it affects: Anyone who used the original BoardStat's numbers for those boards from 2017 to November 2019.

More detail: Marble Hill · Community Board 1 · 311 Service Requests 2010 to 2019

Update, November 21, 2019: The Community Board 1 issue was closed that day with the note that it had been fixed.
